How the engine runs

Three moving parts, one operator.

▶

Acquisition graded on profit, not platform math.

Meta, Google, TikTok and the emerging AI surfaces run out of one plan, with account structure, budget and creative pipeline set against contribution margin. Platform-reported ROAS is a diagnostic, never the scoreboard.

  • One account plan across Meta, Google and TikTok
  • Budget set against new-customer contribution margin
  • Post-purchase attribution, not platform self-scoring
▶

Creative and landing pages tested as one unit.

Ad creative is paired with the page it lands on and tested together, so a winning hook isn’t wasted on a template that can’t convert it. Abe CRO runs the page side of the test at theme and template level.

  • Creative pipeline built for weekly test volume
  • Landing pages tested at template level
  • Winning combinations rolled out, not just reported
▶

Merchandising and promo planned with the media.

Assortment, pricing architecture and the promo calendar are set alongside the media plan — the unglamorous planning work that decides whether a strong quarter is also a profitable one.

  • Promo architecture that protects margin
  • Assortment and allocation planned with demand
  • Weekly business review on your calendar
