The model

Four things that make us not‑an‑agency.

None of this is a service line. It’s how the engagement is built, and it’s the part clients tell us they actually notice.

▶

Embedded in your tools, on your calendar.

We work in your Slack, not a ticketing portal. We join your standups, run your weekly business review, and work to your merchandising and promo calendar — the same rhythm your team already runs. You do not brief us; you talk to us.

  • In your Slack, not a ticket queue
  • On your standups and weekly business review
  • Working to your calendar, not our sprint
▶

Whole department or surgical gap-fill.

Some clients hand us their entire eCom function. Others bring us in to plug one seat: a paid media lead, an email production line, a CRO program, an ERP integration. Same senior people either way, scoped to whatever is actually missing.

  • Embedded in your Slack, calendars and standups
  • Scale up, down or sideways without re-hiring
  • One contract — no procurement gauntlet per role
▶

Headed by people who’ve sat in your seat — for 20+ years.

Every engagement is led by a principal who has personally owned the eCommerce P&L inside major brands — Bloomingdale’s, FAO Schwarz, Hunter Boots, Bliss, Nine West, Nestlé, Hearst and more. They know the calendar, the trade-offs and the weight of a missed forecast because they have carried it themselves.

  • Operator-led, not account-manager-led
  • Strategy that survives a real P&L review
  • No relay between sales, AM and execution
▶

Profit, customers and brand — plus the brass tacks.

We grade ourselves on profitability, new-customer acquisition and brand health — not just channel-reported ROAS. And we still do the unglamorous, results-driving work most agencies skip: planning, allocation, merchandising calendars, promo architecture.

  • One P&L view across marketing, CRO and ops
  • Planning, allocation & merchandising rigor
  • 30–40% average profit lift post-switch

The engagement

What the first ninety days look like.

▶ Week one

Access, audit, priorities.

We get into the accounts rather than asking you to describe them. By the end of week one you have a written teardown and a ranked list of what we are going to fix first.

  • Storefront, media, lifecycle & data audit
  • Ranked opportunities with expected impact
  • Named principal and working channel live

▶ Steady state

A rhythm, not a retainer.

Standups in your Slack, a weekly business review against one P&L view, and work shipped on your calendar. Nothing waits for a monthly call to surface.

  • Weekly business review on your numbers
  • Day-to-day queue triaged and closed in days
  • One contribution-margin view across channels

▶ As you grow

Scale without re-hiring.

Add a seat when a quarter demands it, drop it when it does not. No new contract per role, no recruitment cycle, no ramp-up on a team that already knows your business.

  • Add or drop seats inside one contract
  • No recruiting or onboarding cycle
  • Same senior people throughout
